Steffes Schakt

A responsive marketing and recruitment site for a Stockholm groundwork contractor, built in a CMS so the team can manage it themselves.

RoleFrontend Developer
ClientSteffes Schakt AB, via Webblix
Year2026
StackCMS · HTML · CSS · JS
Visit live site ↗
Overview

Steffes Schakt AB has run groundwork and excavation projects across Stockholm since 1993, specialising in long-reach excavators.

Their work spans water and sewage (VA), infrastructure, dredging and remediation. They needed a modern, responsive website that presents their services and machine park clearly, builds trust with larger clients, and helps attract new operators through job listings, all editable by the team without a developer.

The challenge

The content is varied: four service categories, a large fleet of machines each with its own reach and tonnage, partner logos, a careers area and a contact flow. It all had to stay easy to scan on a phone out in the field, load quickly with a lot of photography, and be manageable through a CMS so the client could update machines and open positions on their own.

What I built
  1. 01

    Responsive frontend

    Built the full layout from the design so that long headings, service cards and the machine gallery reflow cleanly from wide desktop down to mobile.

  2. 02

    Services & company story

    Structured the four service areas (VA, infrastructure, dredging, remediation) and the company history into clear, scannable sections with a consistent visual rhythm.

  3. 03

    Machine-park gallery

    Implemented the machine park as a filterable gallery, each excavator type paired with its own photo and reach and tonnage spec, so clients can quickly find the right machine for a job.

  4. 04

    Contact form with custom success state

    Wired up the enquiry form with GDPR consent and replaced the default submit message with a custom confirmation popup, so people get clear feedback that their message was received.

  5. 05

    Performance & accessibility

    Kept the build lightweight: semantic markup, keyboard-navigable components, and optimised, lazy-loaded images so the site stays fast despite heavy photography.

Outcome

The result is a clean, professional presence that reflects more than 30 years of experience, makes the service range and fleet easy to understand, and gives the team a CMS they can keep up to date on their own.
