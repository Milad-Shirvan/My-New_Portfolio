Power Hub

A corporate site for an infrastructure group, built to introduce the companies it owns and open a clear line to its leadership.

RoleFrontend Developer
ClientPower Hub AB, via Webblix
Year2026
StackCMS · HTML · CSS · JS
Visit live site ↗
Overview

Power Hub AB brings together specialist companies in power, energy, fiber and groundwork under one roof, as part of the Infralogistic group.

As a holding company, its site has a focused job: explain what the group does, introduce the companies it owns, put a face to the leadership team, and make it easy to get in touch. The tone had to feel established and trustworthy rather than flashy.

The challenge

A holding company has little to "sell" visually, so the design had to carry weight through restraint: clear hierarchy, generous space and confident typography. The build needed to stay minimal and fast, present the leadership team cleanly, and route enquiries through a reliable, spam-protected contact form.

What I built
  1. 01

    Responsive corporate layout

    Built a clean, minimal layout that leans on hierarchy and whitespace, holding up from wide desktop down to mobile without losing its composed feel.

  2. 02

    Leadership & group companies

    Structured the leadership profiles with photos and direct contact details, and linked out to the group companies so visitors can navigate the wider organisation.

  3. 03

    Contact form with consent & reCAPTCHA

    Implemented the enquiry form with GDPR consent and reCAPTCHA to keep submissions clean, so the team receives real leads rather than spam.

  4. 04

    Performance & accessibility

    Kept the page lightweight and semantic, with accessible, keyboard-friendly navigation and optimised assets for fast loads.

Outcome

The finished site gives Power Hub a credible, professional presence that matches its role in critical infrastructure, makes the leadership approachable, and channels enquiries cleanly to the team.
